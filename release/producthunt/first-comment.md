# First Comment

Hi Product Hunt.

I built Farpy because render farms can feel like a black box: upload a scene, wait, then hope the output and billing make sense.

Farpy is a Retail Alpha for small Blender and Octane render packages:

- upload a package,
- track it,
- download a ZIP,
- keep a SHA-256 delivery receipt.

The goal is not to claim massive scale on day one. The goal is to make the first small render understandable, priced, and verifiable.

I want brutal feedback from artists:

- What was confusing?
- Would you trust the receipt?
- Did payment feel clear?
- What would stop you from sending a second package?

Please start with a small file. No client-confidential work yet.
