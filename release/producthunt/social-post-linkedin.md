Farpy Retail Alpha is ready for cautious external feedback.

Farpy helps artists send small Blender and Octane render packages, track delivery, download a ZIP, and keep a SHA-256 delivery receipt.

This is not positioned as a large-studio render farm replacement yet. The current goal is clarity, trust, and proof for small packages: previews, tests, splash screens, and short frame ranges.

Looking for practical feedback from Blender and Octane users.
