# Maker Story

Farpy started from a simple frustration: render output, payment, and proof are usually split across too many systems.

The early version focuses on one promise:

Send a small render package, track it, download the result, and keep a receipt.

The product language uses package delivery because that is what customers are trying to understand: did my file arrive, is it being processed, is my ZIP ready, and can I prove what I received?

This launch is intentionally bounded. Farpy is not presenting itself as a studio-scale replacement yet. Retail Alpha is for small Blender and Octane packages, clear pricing, and honest proof.
